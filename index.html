What we do

If you can draw it,
we can mill it.

Non-standard doors. Lock-mitered beams. A profile matched to the 1920s trim already in the house. An arch shared across a door and its sidelites.

Most millwork shops prefer standard sizes and standard catalogs. We are set up for the opposite: a team of craftspeople, drafters and estimators who take a sketch, a spec or a Pinterest screenshot and turn it into something a builder can install. And the same standard runs through every simple piece of trim we send out.

Why Root River

We don't just take the order.
We solve the details.

Custom knives, in-house

If your profile isn't in our library, we grind the knife to match your design. Historic match or modern original.

The shapes catalogs can't do

Drawings before production

We prepare detailed profile drawings for your review and approval before production begins. Our CAD support helps ensure dimensions and design details are right before the first cut.

Clarity before production.

Samples you can hold

For larger orders, or upon request, we can provide physical samples for review before production. This allows you to evaluate the profile and details firsthand before moving forward.

Available for qualifying projects or by request.

Complex geometry

From radius and curved millwork to intricate profiles and lock-miter assemblies, we have the technical capabilities and experience to take on demanding architectural details.

Solutions for challenging designs.

Dedicated specialists

Our team brings together hands-on woodworking experience and technical knowledge. We work closely with you throughout the process, helping move your project from initial drawings to finished millwork.

Experience at every stage.

Hardware & code expertise

Custom projects often involve more than the millwork itself. We coordinate technical details, backing and installation requirements, and communication with onsite trades to help keep everything aligned.
